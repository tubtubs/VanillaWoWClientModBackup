Backup of many client for Vanilla WoW, the 1.12.1 client. May also work on Turtle WoW or other custom servers.
Includes readmes, documentation/wiki clones and some compiled releases. Usually it'll be the latest release as of last update of this backup.

The links below are to the backups in this repo, however the Original Repo links will bring you to the last known maintainer. Alternate Mirrors may also be linked.
